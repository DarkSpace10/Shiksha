End-to-End Integrity

The 10-Step Mandatory Workflow

BookBridge eliminates waste, fraud, and misallocation by requiring physical quality checks, verified institutional student records, and audited allocations.

1

Phase 1: Donation & Organization Review

Step 1: Donor Registration & Listing

Donor uploads book specifications, academic category, condition notes, and multiple photographs. The donor selects a certified partner NGO covering their area.

Step 2: NGO Review & Decision

Authorized NGO staff inspects the digital listing. The NGO can approve, reject, or request clearer photographs/corrections from the donor.

2

Phase 2: Pickup & Physical Quality Inspection

Step 3: Pickup Scheduling

NGO assigns a designated collection volunteer or courier agent with a confirmed time slot.

Step 4: Book Collection

The book is collected from the donor's address and transported to the NGO facility.

Step 5: Physical Inspection & SKU

NGO inspector checks binding, intact pages, and wear. If passed, a unique inventory tracking SKU is assigned.

3

Phase 3: Student Eligibility & Atomic Allocation

Step 6: Catalogue Publication

The verified book is published into the searchable catalog. Students search by course, subject, or academic level.

Step 7: Student Request & Purpose

Students explain how the textbook aligns with their syllabus and submit enrollment evidence.

Step 8: NGO Need Verification

NGO verifies academic relevance and genuine financial need. Auto-allocations are strictly prevented.

4

Phase 4: Concurrency-Safe Allocation & Delivery

Step 9: Concurrency-Safe Allocation

Using database row-level locking (`select_for_update`), the book is allocated and a tracked delivery job is created.

Step 10: Tracked Delivery & Receipt Confirmation

Book is dispatched to student address. The student clicks "Confirm Receipt" upon delivery, completing the audit trail.

Alternate Workflow Handling
• Correction Requested: Donor updates photos/details without starting over.
• Failed Pickup: Recorded with reason, ready for rescheduling.
• Inspection Failed: Heavy wear/damaged books rejected before cataloguing.
• Waitlisted: High-demand textbooks placed on waitlist for upcoming copies.
• Failed Delivery: Address issue flagged and handled with student.
• Cancelled: Donors can safely cancel before pickup is completed.
