About BookBridge

A multi-tenant, organization-isolated book distribution ecosystem built to guarantee that every donated book bridges an educational gap.

Our Mission

Thousands of quality school and college textbooks sit idle on bookshelves, while ambitious students struggle to afford syllabus books essential for their examinations. Traditional donation drives frequently result in dumpsters of mismatched, damaged books because of zero quality control. BookBridge solves this by turning non-profits into certified quality-assurance centers and verified distributors.

Organization Isolation & Security

BookBridge provides multi-NGO tenancy within a unified PostgreSQL architecture. Every operational record — donation listings, pickup logistics, warehouse shelf inventories, student verification files, and delivery jobs — is strictly constrained to the authorized NGO organization. Staff from NGO A cannot inspect, alter, or access records belonging to NGO B.

Zero Auto-Allocations Policy

To maintain absolute accountability to book donors, students do not receive books automatically upon request. Certified NGO verification officers independently verify institutional enrollment cards, syllabus relevance, and genuine need before committing an inventory copy.
