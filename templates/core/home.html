The Verified Donation-to-Delivery Pipeline

A closed-loop system ensuring educational integrity and transparent logistics

1. Donor Listing & Photos

Donors list syllabus books with multiple photos, condition details, and choose an authorized local NGO.

2. NGO Pickup & QA

NGO staff review the donation, assign collection agents, and physically inspect pages and binding before cataloguing.

3. Student Verification

Students upload college/school ID & syllabus requirements. NGO staff verify academic need before approval.

4. Tracked Delivery

Books are allocated via atomic concurrency locking, dispatched with tracking numbers, and receipt is confirmed.
